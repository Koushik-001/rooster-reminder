Privacy Policy

Rooster Reminder

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rooster Reminder ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

Information We Collect

Rooster Reminder does not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on external servers.

The reminders and other information you enter into the App are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us.

How Your Information Is Used

Information entered into Rooster Reminder is used only to provide the reminder functionality of the App, including displaying your reminders and providing reminder notifications.

Data Storage

Your reminder data is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this information and do not maintain a copy of it on our servers.

Analytics and Advertising

Rooster Reminder does not use analytics services, advertising services, or third-party tracking technologies to track your activity.

Accounts

Rooster Reminder does not require you to create an account or provide an email address, phone number, password, or other account information.

Payments

Rooster Reminder does not currently offer paid features, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Third-Party Services

Rooster Reminder does not share your reminder data with third-party services.

Data Security

Because your reminder information is stored locally on your device, the security of that information also depends on the security of your device and its operating system.

Children's Privacy

Rooster Reminder does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us through the contact information provided on the App's store listing.
